Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Free gives you 50 replies a day on WhatsApp and Telegram, forever. Every new shop also gets 7 days of Pro free, and no card is needed.

Will Velo make up prices?

No. Velo only quotes prices and products from your catalog. If something is missing, it hands the chat to you instead of guessing.

Can I take over a chat myself?

Yes. Open the chat in your inbox and reply. Velo steps back for that chat until you hand it back.

Does it speak Pidgin?

Yes. Velo answers the way each customer writes: Pidgin in, Pidgin out; formal English in, formal English out.

What is Chat with Velo?

Your own assistant inside the app. Ask it about your products and prices, or ask it to write a caption or a reply. It can also break down your week (chats, recorded sales, busiest days) and make a receipt when you tell it what was sold.

How do I pay?

By card, bank transfer or USSD on a secure Paystack or Flutterwave checkout. We never see or store your full card number.
