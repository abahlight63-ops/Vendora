Getting started

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Free gives you 50 replies a day on WhatsApp and Telegram, forever. Every new shop also gets 7 days of Pro free, and no card is needed.

How do I connect my WhatsApp?

Open Channels in the app and tap Connect on WhatsApp. A Meta window opens: sign in with Facebook, pick your business number and you are done.

How do I add my products?

Add them one by one in Catalog, import a list, or send a message from your own number like "LEARN: Blue gown ₦45,000". Velo saves the name and price.

How long does setup take?

Most shops are live in under 30 minutes: add a few products, connect a channel, then try Velo in Test Drive.

Plans and billing

How much does VeloSales Ai cost?

Free is ₦0. Plus is ₦7,499 a month for 500 replies a day. Pro is ₦14,999 a month for 1,500 replies a day, voice notes and the smartest AI. Business starts from ₦39,999 a month.

How do I pay?

By card, bank transfer or USSD on a secure Paystack or Flutterwave checkout. We never see or store your full card number.

What happens when I reach my daily limit?

Customers get a polite "we will reply shortly" message and the chat is flagged for you. Your limit resets at midnight, or you can upgrade any time.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Stop paying at any time and you keep your plan until the end of what you paid for, then move to Free. Your first payment can be refunded within 7 days if the service did not work for you.

How Velo answers

Will Velo make up prices?

No. Velo only quotes prices and products from your catalog. If something is missing, it hands the chat to you instead of guessing.

What happens when Velo is not sure?

It tells the customer you will reply shortly, flags the chat as "Needs you" in your inbox and alerts you, so you can step in.

Does it speak Pidgin?

Yes. Velo answers the way each customer writes: Pidgin in, Pidgin out; formal English in, formal English out.

Can I take over a chat myself?

Yes. Open the chat in your inbox and reply. Velo steps back for that chat until you hand it back.

What is Chat with Velo?

Your own assistant inside the app. Ask it about your products and prices, or ask it to write a caption or a reply. It can also break down your week (chats, recorded sales, busiest days) and make a receipt when you tell it what was sold.

Channels and data

Which apps does Velo work on?

WhatsApp and Telegram today. Facebook Messenger and Instagram are coming soon. One inbox shows them all.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your catalog and chats are used only to answer your customers. We never sell data, show ads, or train public AI models on your content.
