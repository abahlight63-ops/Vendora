How much does VeloSales Ai cost?

Free is ₦0. Plus is ₦7,499 a month for 500 replies a day. Pro is ₦14,999 a month for 1,500 replies a day, voice notes and the smartest AI. Business starts from ₦39,999 a month.

How do I pay?

By card, bank transfer or USSD on a secure Paystack or Flutterwave checkout. We never see or store your full card number.

What happens when I reach my daily limit?

Customers get a polite "we will reply shortly" message and the chat is flagged for you. Your limit resets at midnight, or you can upgrade any time.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Stop paying at any time and you keep your plan until the end of what you paid for, then move to Free. Your first payment can be refunded within 7 days if the service did not work for you.
