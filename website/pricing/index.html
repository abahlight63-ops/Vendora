Billing questions

When am I charged?

When you choose a plan in the app, on a secure checkout page. Nothing is charged during the 7-day trial, and the free plan never asks for a card.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from Billing. Your plan runs to the end of the paid period, then the account drops to free — catalog, inbox and history stay exactly where they are.

What about refunds?

First payment: within 7 days if the service genuinely did not work for you. Duplicate or failed charges are always refunded. Write from the in-app Help page or contact support.

Which currency?

Naira by default, dollars if you prefer. Both are shown at checkout, so the card statement matches what you agreed to.

Not sure which one?

Start free, watch a week of real chats, then decide with numbers instead of guesses. Or ask us — we will tell you if you do not need the upgrade.
