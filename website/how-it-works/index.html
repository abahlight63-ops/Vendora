Set up

Three steps, one afternoon

  1. Add your productsType them in Catalog, import a list, or send "LEARN: Blue gown ₦45,000" from your own number. Velo saves the name and price.
  2. Connect your channelsWhatsApp through a Meta window, Telegram with a bot code, Messenger and Instagram through your Facebook page.
  3. Test, then switch onAsk Velo questions like a customer in Test Drive. When you are happy with the answers, go live.

Every message

What happens when a customer writes

1. The message arrives

From WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger or Instagram, into one inbox.

2. Velo checks your catalog

Your products, prices, opening hours and the last messages in the chat.

3. Velo replies

In seconds, in English or Pidgin, using only your facts.

4. Or hands it to you

Not sure? The customer hears you will reply shortly, and you get an alert.
