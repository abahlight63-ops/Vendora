Trial questions, answered

Do I need a card?

No. Sign up with your email or Google account, verify the code, and the trial is already running.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. There is no card on file during the trial, so there is nothing to charge. Payment only happens when you pick a plan yourself.

Can I keep my catalog if I downgrade?

Yes. Products, conversations and history stay. You lose the higher daily allowance and the Pro-only automations, not your data.

How do I pay after the trial?

By card on a secure checkout from the Billing page. If your card gives trouble, message support and we will sort out the next step with you.

Seven days is enough to know

If your shop is quieter than you thought, stay free. If the bot is carrying real conversations, you will feel it by day three.
