THE WHOLE MACHINE, IN PLAIN WORDS

How VeloSales Ai answers your WhatsApp

You already sell in chat. VeloSales Ai takes the repeat questions — price, stock, delivery, opening hours — and answers them in seconds from your catalog, in the customer's own language. Everything else comes to you.

Start free — 7 days Pro See what each plan includes

No card · Works with a sandbox number first · Your catalog stays yours

1

Connect a WhatsApp number

Start in the sandbox — a test number the app gives you — so nothing touches your real shop until you are ready. When it looks right, open Connect, tap WhatsApp and finish Meta's Embedded Signup with your business number.

Telegram is there too, using the same catalog and the same AI settings.

2

Teach it your catalog

Two honest ways in. Add products on the Catalog page with a name, price, description and photo — or text LEARN: Blue gown ₦45,000 from your owner number and let the AI file it. Same name twice updates the price instead of creating a duplicate.

On Pro, paste your WhatsApp Business profile and Profile SYNC scaffolds the list for you.

3

Pick how it thinks

In AI settings you choose the tier that answers: Fast for quick price checks, Normal for everyday selling, Smart when a question needs careful reading. You are choosing a behaviour, not a brand — the app keeps the machinery out of your way.

Change it any time; the next message uses the new tier.

4

Let it sell, then check the flags

The bot answers around the clock. When it is not sure, it does not guess: it sends a polite handoff, flags the chat in your inbox and alerts your personal WhatsApp with the customer's exact words. You close the ones that matter.

Your daily digest sums up chats, flags and sales signals in one read.

ONE NIGHT, ONE SHOP

What it actually looks like

23:48 — a customer asks

"Abeg, blue gown still dey? How much for delivery to Lekki?"

VeloSales Ai replies in seconds with the price, the sizes in stock and the delivery fee — taken from the catalog you taught it, in the same Pidgin the customer used.

23:49 — a bigger question

"I need 50 pieces for aso-ebi, can you do a discount?"

That is a judgement call, not a price lookup. The bot asks for a name, flags the chat hot and alerts you — so the first thing you read in the morning is a warm lead, not a missed sale.

It never invents a price. If the catalog does not contain the answer, the bot hands over instead of guessing. That rule is the product, not a setting.

BEFORE YOU START

What you need

A WhatsApp number

A business number you can receive a code on. The sandbox works before you connect it.

Your products and prices

Even a rough list is enough. Ten products with real prices beat a hundred invented ones.

Fifteen minutes

That is the honest setup time for a first catalog. You can refine it later from your phone.
